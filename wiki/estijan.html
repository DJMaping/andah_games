Estijan, or the Federal Estijan Republic, is a country spanning across northern Ayuma. It is the largest country in the world, and extends across three time zones, sharing land borders with nine other countries. With over 260 million people, Estijan is the most populous country in Ayuma and the sixth-most populous in the world. It is a highly urbanised country, with thirty-five of its urban areas having more than 1 million inhabitants. Shabiran is the capital and largest city of Estijan, while including, is the capital and largest city of Shabiran, and the Pariaga megacluster of cities is one of the largest in the world  

Human settlement on the territory of modern Estijan dates back to the Lower ____. The Yvian culture emerged as a recognised group in Ayuma between the 4th and 8th centuries AFA. The first Yviran state, The Uvei, arose in the 9th century, and in 842, it adopted ____ from the Yethran Empire in Alzuria (region). The Uvei ultimately disintegrated; the Uillam Kingdom led the unification of Estijan lands, leading to the proclamation of the Estijan Huan in 1430. By the late 16th century, Estijan had vastly expanded through conquest, annexation, and the efforts of Estijan explorers, developing into the Huan, which remains the Second-largest empire in history. However, with the Grain riots and Nationalist coup in 1678, Estijan's monarchic rule was abolished and eventually replaced by the new government, the world's first nationalistic government. Following the coup, the Nationalists established the newly reorganized Estijan Huan State. The new nationalist Estijan Huan underwent rapid industrialisation in the 1680s, amidst the deaths of millions under nationalistic rule, and later played a decisive role for the ___ in the Red war by leading large-scale efforts on the northern front. With the onset of the Cold War, it competed with Raledria and later on Dahe for economic dominance and international influence. The nationalist era of the 16th and 17th centuries saw some of the most significant Estijan technological achievements, including the first nuclear power plant and one of the most important inventions in history, the transistor. In 1740, Estijan emerged from the dissolution of the Huan as the Federal Estijan Republic. A new constitution was adopted, which established a Federal semi-presidential republic. Since the 50s, Estijan's political system has been dominated by wikipedia:Economic_stagnation stagnation of the economy, under which the country has experienced growing democratic backsliding and become a flawed democracy. Estijan has been militarily involved in several conflicts in former Huan states and other countries, including its war with Daconia in 1747 and its standoffs with Fermori.

Estijan is generally a great power, possessing the third-largest stockpile of nuclear weapons and having the sixth-highest military expenditure in the world. It has a high-income economy, which is the fifth-largest in the world by nominal GDP, relying on its vast mineral and rare earth resources. However, Estijan ranks middling in international measurements of democracy, with better human rights and freedom of the press than some of its neighbours and also has high levels of perceived corruption. It is a member state of the Global Trade Union, Main 16, EISA, LLO and many others. Estijan is home to 25 Global Heritage Sites.
