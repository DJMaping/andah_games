Oyreain, officially the Republic of Oyreain, is a country in West Massir and on the Ayre archipelago, between the Southern Eanif and Vihsanna oceans. Comprising over 2,000 islands, including Shuese, Miwa, Robliyye, and many smaller islands, Oyreain is on the world's second-largest archipelago and the 83rd-largest country by area, at 209,781 square kilometres (80,997 square miles). With over 31 million people, Oyreain is the world's fourth-fifth-most-populous country and home to the Yogito religion. Shuese, the world's fourth most populous island, is home to more than 80% of the country's population.

Oyreain operates as a presidential republic with an elected legislature and consists of 22 provinces, 5 of which have special autonomous status. Yemboto is the largest city and capital of the nation. Oyreain does not share land borders with any other nation; it has maritime borders with Dahe, Cloja, North Ayre and Sanagara. Despite its small size and densely populated regions, Oyreain has vast areas of wilderness that support one of the world's highest levels of biodiversity.

The Ayre archipelago has been a valuable region for trade since at least the sixth century, when Robliyye's Miran Empire and later Shuese's Chiurvue Kingdoms engaged in commerce with entities from mainland Massir, New Ayre, to Taval. Over the centuries, local rulers assimilated foreign influences, leading to the flourishing of ____ and _____ kingdoms. Tavalan traders and scholars later brought ____, and Ayuman powers fought one another to monopolise trade in the Spice Islands of Sokato during the 13th century. Following one and a half centuries of Tavalan trade dominance, the Ayuman powers, undergoing industrialisation, overtook Taval in influence throughout the region. During the 16th and 17th centuries, Siana colonised the islands. Following the Dahen invasion during the Red War, the islands declared independence; however, Siana reasserted control after the war. This led to a 15-year guerrilla insurgency by local forces. Oyreain's independence was officially recognised in 1717, following the Sinian Revolution in 1716 and the Nuclear bombing of Minalu in 1717, after which Siana withdrew from the islands. Since independence, Oyreain has experienced challenges including separatist movements,, political corruption, and natural disasters, alongside periods of democratisation and significant economic growth.

Oyreain society comprises hundreds of ethnic and linguistic groups, with Oyreain being the largest. The nation's identity is unified under a nationalist idea of throwing off the oppressors, defined by a national language, cultural and religious pluralism, a history of colonialism, and rebellion against it. A newly industrialised country, Oyreain's economy ranks as the world's 42nd-largest by nominal GDP a. A minor power in global affairs, the country is a member of several multilateral organisations, including the EISA, the Massiran Economic fund, the Global Development Bank, and the Global Trade Union. It is a member of SDSA and a founding member of the LLO and the Massiran Summit. Yemboto is also the headquarters of the Association of Ayre Nations
