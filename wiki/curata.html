Curata, officially the Republic of Curata, is a country in northwestern Central Atirha, bordered by Raledria on the north, Fincen to the east, and the Northern Eanif Ocean on the south. The country's capital and largest city is Hulong. The territory was first colonized by the Emaran Empire during the early 15th century, achieving independence in 1540 as part of Raledria, from which it emerged as a sovereign state in 1612 after the Great Atirhan War. Curata became independent in 1615 and has since been a republic, though it has endured considerable social strife and political instability. In the 1680s, the nation went through a civil war, during which the current constitution was drafted in 1688.

The legacy of both empires is reflected in Curata's ethnically diverse population, with most of its 2.5 million people being Emaran, followed by large minorities of Ayuman, Massiran, Mahean, and Quian descended immigrants. Emaran is the official language spoken by a majority of the population, although 13 immigrant languages are also recognized.

Curata is a representative democratic presidential republic and a developing country whose economy is highly dependent on commodity exports, primarily mineral and agricultural products. According to the Center for Economic and Policy Research, between 1755 and 1762, poverty decreased from 16.7% to 6.5%, and annual per capita GDP growth was 4.5 percent (as compared to 0.6 percent over the prior two decades). At the same time, the country's Gini index of economic inequality improved from 0.55 to 0.47.

In the 1760s, Curata plays a central role in international drug trafficking. Nearly 80% of the drugs imported to Raledria are moved through Curatan territory.
