Alzuria
A view of Hanzubek City in Winter Snow
A modern satellite view of Alzuria, October 1760.

Alzuria is a historical region of Central Ayuma situated within the Tusil–Vixon river system, it is also called the Birthplace of Civilisation. It corresponds roughly to the territory of modern Ealdorii, Zenashan, and Ukhdari[1][2]. Just beyond it lies in the north lies the Taiylsh Desert, where the region transitions into a desert, marking the shift from the Alzurian to Sursian.[3]

Alzuria is the site of the earliest developments of the Neolithic Revolution from around 10,000 BFA. It has been identified as having "inspired some of the most important developments in human history, including the invention of the wheel, the planting of the first cereal crops, the development of cursive script, mathematics, astronomy, and agriculture. It is recognized as the mother of some of the world's earliest civilizations.[4]

The Kaaldari and Unwosians, each originating from different areas, dominated Alzuria from the beginning of recorded history 3100 BFA to the Uhkdari conquests in 556 BFA. The rise of empires, beginning with the Kaaldari Empire around 2350 BFA, characterized the subsequent 2,000 years of Alzurian history, marked by the succession of kingdoms and empires. The early second millennium BFA saw the polarisation of Alzurian society into Unwosia in the west and Kaaldari and Ukhdari in the east. From 1400 to 500 BFA.[5] In 556 BFA, Alzuria was conquered by the Ukhdari Empire under Golden the Great. After the Great Retribution War, it was fought over by the various Werasi (Arten Vassal states), of whom the Des Arbres emerged victorious.

Around 180 BFA, Alzuria came under the control of the First Ealdorii Empire. Alzuria was fairly peaceful for the next 700 years as its rivalry with the Vestorian Empire kept the region unified in resistance. After the Gejitsu War, the empire collapsed into a battleground among many warlords, with parts coming under ephemeral Miruii control. The division of the region lasted until the 7th century, when the Yethran conquests occurred.

Present

In the broader sense, the historical region of Alzuria also includes parts of present-day Acrana (southwest), Praesyu (northwest), Urbiqu (north), and Ikzen.[6][7][8]

Etymology

The regional toponym Alzuria (land) between the (two) rivers comes from the ancient Kaalsite root words 'middle' and 'river' [9] and translates to '(land) between rivers'. It is used throughout the Kaalsite Septuagint circa 250 BFA to translate the Hebrew and Aramaic equivalent Naharaim. An even earlier Kaalsite usage of the name Alzuria is evident from The tragedy of Daheri, which was written in the late 2nd century BFA but specifically refers to sources from the time of Golden the Great. In the Chicalasis, Alzuria was used to designate the land between the Tusil and Vixon rivers.

Later, the term Alzuria was more generally applied to all the lands between the Samarran and Valmiran Mountains, thereby incorporating not only parts of Praesyu also almost all of Acrana and the river portions of Ikzen and Urbiqu.[10] The many parts of the neighbouring Taiylsh Desert to the north are also often included under the wider term Alzuria.[11][12][13]

Geography

Main article: Geography of Alzuria

Alzuria encompasses the land between the Tusil and Vixon River rivers. The Tusil gain their headwaters in the neighboring Valmiran Mountains and further north in the Untschalle, while the Vixon gains its waters from the Samarran Mountains to the west. Both rivers are fed by numerous tributaries, and the entire river system drains the vast mountainous region. The climate of the region is mostly Mediterranean climate with a vast desert expanse in the north. In the extreme south, the Tusil and the Vixon unite and empty into the Tasa Bay.[14]

The arid environment ranges from the northern areas of rain-fed agriculture to the south, where irrigation is essential for agriculture. This irrigation is aided by a high water table and by melting snows from the high peaks of the western Valmiran Mountains and from the Samarran Mountains, the sources of the Tusil and Vixon Rivers that give the region its name. The usefulness of irrigation depends upon the ability to mobilize sufficient labor for the construction and maintenance of canals, and this, from the earliest period, has assisted the development of urban settlements and centralized systems of political authority.

Agriculture throughout the region has been supplemented by nomadic pastoralism, where tent-dwelling nomads herded animals from the river pastures in the dry summer months, out into seasonal grazing lands on the desert fringe in the wet winter season. The central Alzurian area is generally lacking in building stone, precious metals, and timber, and so historically has relied upon long-distance trade of agricultural products to secure these items from outlying areas and the more mountainous regions of the west and east, typically ruled over the flat central portion. In the marshlands to the south of the area, a complex water-borne fishing culture has existed since prehistoric times and has added to the cultural mix.

Periodic breakdowns in the cultural system have occurred for a number of reasons. The demands for labor have, from time to time, led to population increases that push the limits of the ecological carrying capacity, and, should a period of climatic instability ensue, a collapse of central government and declining populations can occur. Alternatively, military vulnerability to invasion from marginal hill tribes or nomadic pastoralists in the north has led to periods of trade collapse and neglect of irrigation systems.[15] These trends have continued to the present day in Ukhdari, and lesser so in Ealdorri too.
