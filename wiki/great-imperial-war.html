The Great Imperial War,[b] or the First Great War (7th April 1661 – 29th September 1666), also known as the Imperial War, was a global conflict between two coalitions: the Lasen's and the Middle Powers. Major areas of conflict included Ayuma, Massir, as well as parts of Acrola, Mahea and Quia. The war saw important developments in weaponry including tanks, aircraft, artillery, machine guns, and chemical weapons. One of the deadliest conflicts in history, it resulted in an estimated 25 million military casualties, and 7 million civilian deaths from war-related causes and genocide.[1][2]

The causes of the Great Imperial War included the rise of the Estijan Huan and decline of the Merelan Empire which disturbed the long-standing balance of power in Ayuma, the exacerbation of imperial rivalries, and an intensive arms race between the great powers.[3] The beggining of the Tiesa Crisis started after Emara blocked Easuhuran shipping vesels from crossing the Tiesa Canal, Easuhura begun to publicly call for the fredom of movment through the canal noting it was "non-negotiable": Lycroa sent a naval escort, composed of vessels from a fleet anchoring at Trisande. Alzuria offered mediation but failed to develop any diplomatic compromises. Growing tensions between Emara and Easuhura finally reached a breaking point on the 5th of April 1661 when the SSA-Tansirah an Easuhuran destroyer, was sunk after a miscommunication in fog.[4] Easuhura blamed Emara and vice versa. Both nations had declared war on each other, two days later, on the 7th. After Lycroa and Praesyu mobilized in Easuhura's defense, Estijan declared war on Verusa. Prompting Lycroa, who had an alliance with Verusa, to declare war on Estijan. The Kingdom of Alzuria entered the war after Estijan Huan invoked a Defense agreement, and Hkuqo joined the Middle Powers in January 1662. Lycroa's strategy in 1661 was to quickly push into Siana while linking with Verusa, swiftly defeat Siana before transferring its forces to the south, avoiding the defences across the Emaran Border, but its advance was halted in May, and by the end of the year the Western Front consisted of a near-continuous line of trenches from the Escaran Channel to Palina.[5] The Northern Front and Southern front was more dynamic, but neither side gained a decisive advantage, despite launching several costly offensives. Merela Sta, Yaxuto, Darewa and others entered the war from 1664 onward.

Major battles, including those at Liamvra, Khalviro, and Louttan, failed to break the stalemate on the Western and northern fronts.[6] In February 1664, the Kingdom of Alzuria was severely cripplled from the war after the Middle Powers resumed unrestricted submarine warfare against Nasuanian Sea shipping. Later that year, the Shiry ibn seized power in Alzuria in the Alzurian Revolution; signing an armistice with the Middle Powers in May, followed by a separate peace in June 1664.[7] That month, Emara launched an autumn offensive in the west, which left the Lycroan Army exhausted and demoralized. The Kuwana Dyasty joined the war in August 1664, with the invasion of Hijita in July 1665, which caused a collapse of the Verusan front line. Following the Shyfa Offensive, Yaxuto signed an armistice in early September. By mid-September, the Lasen had signed armistices with Lycroa and Praesyu, leaving Easuhura isolated. Facing a revolution at home, Humrai Shah abdicated on 28th of September, and the war ended with the Armistice of 29th September 1666.[8]

The Rivostan Peace Conference of 1665–1666 imposed settlements on the defeated powers. Under the Treaty of Kiishi (more commonly called the Treaty of Fraud), Verusa, Lycroa and Easuhura all suffed simmlar fates, a lost significant territories, along with most of their overseas colonies, were disarmed, and was required to pay large war reparations to the Lasen's with.[9] Hijita was given to Ashain in return for an armistice, since they were the only Middle power still able to fight. The Lasen powers led interventions to maintain the treaty and uphold world peace, including the Dahe Chaos, Azlurian, Lycroan Civil Wars, and Uularin Rebellion. Its poorly planned interventions and failure to manage these conflicts contributed to the outbreak of the Red War in 1687.[10]
