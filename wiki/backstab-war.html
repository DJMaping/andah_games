The Backstab War was a conflict which led to the secession of the southern provinces (mainly the former Southern Laselteh) from the Republic of Laselteh and the establishment of an independent First Republic of Grazail.

The people of the south were mainly Vaver and Dabdan. Both peoples were traditionally ___ as contrasted with ____-dominated people of the north. Many outspoken liberals, including the Grazail family from which the nation is named, regarded Tyidai Idueria rule as despotic. There were high levels of unemployment and industrial unrest among the working classes.[3]

On 22 January 1654, riots erupted in Mushira and shops were looted. Theatregoers who had just watched the nationalistic opera Lio Eulaellom Isur joined the mob. Uprisings followed elsewhere in the country. Factories were occupied and machinery destroyed. Order was restored briefly after Idueria committed troops to the Southern Provinces, but rioting continued, and leadership was taken up by radicals, who started talking of secession.[4]

Laseltshen units saw mass desertion of recruits from the southern provinces and pulled out. On 22 March, a newly formed Provisional Government in Mushira declared independence and called for the election of a Union of Grazail.[5]. Following the installation of Haena Grazail as High Minister of the Grazailite state in 1656, Tyidai Idueria made a belated attempt to reconquer Grazail and restore his position through a military campaign. This Campaign failed because of Etirha's military intervention, which pulled in Pelan, which was occupied by Etirha a week later. The Laseltshen High Minister surrendered after the Battle of Raeral was defeated and accepted the declaration of Grazailite independence in 1656 by signing the Treaty of Zayden.
