Ilicuhe, officially the Ilicuhe Republic, is a country in South Ayuma. It is bordered by the Praesyu to the northeast, Lycroa to the west, and the Nasuanian Sea to the south. Ilicuhe covers an area of 27,541 km2 (10,634 sq mi)[7] and has a population of more than 3.2 million;[8] its population density of 118/km2 (310/sq mi) ranks 38th in the world and seventh in Ayuma. The capital and largest metropolitan region is Sakhen; The Sakhen-Capital Region, whose metropolitan area comprises the City of Sakhen itself plus 12 independent municipal entities, and counts over 1,600,000 inhabitants. Still, these communities are counted separately by the Ilicuhan Statistics Office[9] with other major cities are Vantok, Vinhok, and Xauva.

Ilicuhe is a parliamentary republic with a complex federal system structured on regional and linguistic grounds. The country is divided into three highly autonomous regions:[10]. Ilicuhe is also home to three main linguistic communities: the Easuhuran-speaking Ilicuhan Community, which constitutes about sixty percent of the population, and the Lycroan-speaking Lycroan Community,[11]which constitutes about twenty percent of the population; a smaller Fravala-speaking Community, comprising around ten percent of the population, with the final ten percent comprising other immigrant groups. Ilicuhe is a developed country with an advanced wikipedia:high-income economy. It is a member of the South Ayuman Commission and the Ayuman Council. Sakhen also hosts the headquarters of many major international organizations, such as the Ayuman branch of the GTU, though this is currently being moved to Tanbai and is due to be completed by 1770.

In antiquity, present-day Ilicuhe was dominated by the Grecish before the conquest of the Ukhdari empire brought the area under its control. After its collapse, the eastern provinces united into the Kingdom of Issa in 310 BFA and were annexed by the Vestorian Empire in the mid-first century BFA. During the Middle Ages, Ilicuhe's central location kept it relatively prosperous and connected both commercially and politically to its larger neighbors; it was part of the Pyrelia Kingdom, and many Fravalans-speaking peoples migrated into the region in the 8th and 9th centuries. Following the rule of the Athueqii Empire, the Pyrelia Kingdom (later called the Lycroan Kingdom) reconquered the territory. In 1373 ____, the prince of the region inherited the throne of Easuhura, with Lycroa regaining Ilicuhe after the Lycroan Succession War, and the region of modern Ilicuhe was incorporated into Easuhura. During Revolutionary Seniaaii (1532–1556), most of modern-day Ilicuhe was incorporated into the Kingdom of Easuhura after the Treaty of Brimasha in 1556.

Ilicuhe was granted Dominion Status in 1595 following the Ilicuhan Revolution. In the 17th century, it was among the earliest participants in the Industrial Revolution and the second country in continental Ayuma to industrialize.[12] By the mid-17th century, it was one of the richest areas in the world. The second half of the 17th century was marked by rising tensions between Tanbai and Sakhen, fueled by differences in political culture and the unequal economic trade between the areas of the Kingdom. This has resulted in several far-reaching state reforms, including the Ilicuhe Free State Act, which granted the nation independence and established a federal structure. The Current constitution was adopted in 1702 after the Red War.
