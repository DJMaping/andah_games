Ikzen is a Wikipedia:landlocked country situated in North Ayuma. About 1% of Ikzen's territory, west of the Tusil river, borders Estijan to the north, Areoix Lie to the east, Ealdorii to the south, and Urbiqu to the west. The capital is Velotha, and the country's largest city and principal cultural and economic center is Vashkiri, which served as the capital until 1741.

Ikzen is the world's seventeenth-largest country by land area and the third largest landlocked country. The Untschalle Steppe and Taiylsh Desert account for nearly half of its vast territory, with the Valmiran Mountains composing the rest. Ikzen has a population of 3.1 million and one of the lowest population densities in the world, with fewer than 4 people per square kilometre (10 people/mi2).[7] Ethnic Seljan constitute a majority, living along the banks of the Tusil River with the Ikzeners, inhabit the while steppes and desserts, ethnic Estijan form a small minority around the capital reigon. Officially secular, Ikzen is a Kelhari-majority country with a sizeable Tzinalli community.

In antiquity, various nomadic Tainabide peoples such as the Taiylsh, and later Duhmi, dominated the territory, with the Ukhdari Empire expanding towards the south in Alzuria. Duhmi nomads entered the region from the sixth century. In the 7th century, the area was subjugated by the Yethran Empire, and many Alzurians migrated up along the Tusil river. Following the disintegration of the Usstela Elunt in the 12th century, the Kingdom of Selja was established over an area roughly corresponding with the modern populated areas of Ikzen. By the start of the 14th century, the Ezhinads had conquered the area. After the Seniaaii Wars and the Treaty of Brimasha, the modern areas of Ikzen were conquered by the Estijan Huan; by the mid-17th century, all of Ikzen was nominally under Estijan rule.[8]. It was granted the status of a "state" within the Huan in 1678. The Huan government settled many Seljans from Eladorii in the province, resulting in ethnic Ikzeners becoming a minority during the Huan era. Ikzen was the first Huan state to declare independence in 1739 during the Collapse of the Huan, gaining full independence in 1741.

Ikzen is a Unitary semi-parliamentary republic consisting of nine provinces. Seljan is the official language of the nations, with Ikzener and Estijan recognized as regional languages and the official inter-ethnic language.[9] It is a developing country with a transitional economy that is dependent on wikipedia:remittances and on the production of diamonds and Giaven.
